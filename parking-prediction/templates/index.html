How It Works

Four quick steps from choosing a lot to parking in it.

  1. 1

    Choose Location

    Pick the lot or garage you plan to use from the list of parking areas.

  2. 2

    Check Parking

    See how many spaces are open right now and how full the lot is.

  3. 3

    Predict Availability

    ParkPredict compares current occupancy with historical patterns to estimate spaces at your arrival time.

  4. 4

    Find Your Spot

    Head to the lot with the best odds, or leave earlier if it is filling up.

Why ParkPredict?

Most parking tools tell you what is happening right now. ParkPredict also looks ahead to the moment you actually arrive.

  • Current occupancy

    See how full a lot is before you set off.

  • Historical patterns

    Past busy and quiet periods help estimate what is likely later.

  • Arrival-time estimate

    Plan for when you will be there, not just for right now.

  • Clear at a glance

    Three numbers tell the story: spaces open, how full, and what to expect.

  • Works on any screen

    Check from your phone on the way, or from a laptop before you leave.

  • Less circling

    Skip full lots and save time, fuel, and stress.
